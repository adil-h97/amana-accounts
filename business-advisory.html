Business setup, answered.

What specific services does Amana'Accounts provide?

A wide range of business consultancy services, including company formation, PRO services, M&A services and general strategic guidance for both start ups and established businesses across all sectors.

If you need support with anything else and you're unsure if we can help, just contact us and we'll let you know.

How can I set up a business in the UAE?

The process involves choosing the right legal structure (LLC, FZE and so on), a jurisdiction (mainland or a free zone), and obtaining a professional business license, business address, bank account, visas and regulatory approvals.

What are the costs of setting up a business in the UAE?

Costs depend on the business structure, number of visas and office requirements. Typical expenses include license fees (AED 5,000 to 25,000), visa fees (AED 3,000 to 6,000 per visa) and office space, physical or flexi desk.

We can help guide you to the most suitable and cost effective package for you.

How long does it take to set up a company in the UAE?

As quick as one week, or up to around two months, depending on the chosen location, approvals and documentation.

Can a free zone company trade in mainland UAE?

Free zone companies often have restrictions on mainland operations. It may still be possible to operate in mainland UAE, for example through a mainland branch or agent, depending on the company's activities, such as IT, marketing or professional services. Contact us to find out more.

What are common mistakes when starting a business in the UAE?

Typical pitfalls include choosing the wrong location or license type, often at the encouragement of agents with their own interests, underestimating costs, missing required approvals and neglecting professional insurance. We can help guide you through the journey of starting and running your business.

Frequently asked questions

Corporate tax, answered.

What is the UAE corporate tax rate?

9% on taxable income above AED 375,000. Companies operating in the UAE are generally subject to corporate tax.

Do I need to register for UAE corporate tax?

Yes, if you're a company operating in the UAE. Registration is due within three months of incorporation, and late registration carries a AED 10,000 penalty.

Do I have to pay UAE corporate tax?

If your taxable profit is under AED 375,000, you're exempt from paying corporate tax, but you still need to file a tax return, tax computation and financial statements. Skip that and you'll face penalties from the FTA regardless.

Above that threshold, it may still be possible to claim reliefs or reduce your liability through efficient tax structuring.

Do free zone companies need to pay corporate tax?

Most do. Some free zone companies can benefit from a 0% rate, subject to detailed conditions.

What is considered taxable income under UAE corporate tax law?

Net profit as per accounting standards (IFRS), adjusted for specific deductions and exclusions under the tax law.

Are salaries to owners deductible?

Reasonable compensation paid to owners actively involved in the business is generally deductible. This is subject to transfer pricing rules though, so it's worth getting right to avoid penalties.

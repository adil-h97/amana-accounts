Frequently asked questions

VAT, answered.

Do I need to register for VAT?

If your taxable sales revenue exceeds AED 375,000 in a 12 month period, registration is mandatory. Above AED 187,000, you can register voluntarily.

You must register within 30 days of crossing the AED 375,000 threshold, or risk a AED 10,000 late registration penalty.

How often do I need to file VAT returns?

Most businesses file quarterly. Some higher revenue businesses need to file monthly.

What happens if I file my VAT return late?

A penalty of AED 1,000 to 2,000, plus potential percentage penalties on late payment.

Can I reclaim VAT on my costs after becoming VAT registered?

Yes, provided the expenses are valid business costs and meet VAT recovery criteria. Properly managed VAT filings can genuinely save you money.
